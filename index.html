Germany occupational exposure dashboard

Occupations in Germany, mapped by employment and AI impact.

This prototype maps Germany occupations by employment and exposure. Each tile is an occupation, area reflects employment, and color reflects the selected layer. The main emphasis is digital AI exposure, with alternative views for overall AI exposure, automation exposure, and monthly pay.

How to read

Bigger tiles mean more workers. Green means lower impact, yellow is middle, and red means higher impact on the chosen layer.

Method notes

This dashboard uses official German and EU labor statistics for the occupation base layer, then applies our own task-based AI exposure analysis on top.

Official source backbone: Destatis occupation employment tables, Destatis earnings tables, Eurostat LFS occupation data, and KldB 2010 / ISCO classification metadata.

Analytical layer: our AI exposure, automation exposure, and outlook scores are modeled interpretations of occupational task structure, not official statistics.
